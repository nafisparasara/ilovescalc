About iLovesCalc.com

Fast, Accurate & Privacy-First Calculation Tools for Everyone

iLovesCalc.com was created with a simple mission: to make everyday financial planning, health tracking, mathematical problem-solving, and unit conversions effortless, instantaneous, and completely free.

Why We Built iLovesCalc

Many online calculators are slow, overloaded with pop-ups, or require you to enter personal details before showing results. At iLovesCalc, we engineered a clean, responsive suite of 37 specialized calculators that deliver live results as you type—with zero latency and zero sign-ups.

Our Core Principles

  • Verified Formulas: Every financial, health, and mathematical tool is built on transparent, industry-standard equations (such as banking amortization for EMIs, Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR, and WHO standards for BMI), with the exact formula displayed right below every calculator.
  • 100% Browser-Based Privacy: Your numbers stay on your device. All calculations run locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript—we never store or transmit your inputs to external databases.
  • Accessibility & Speed: Designed for mobile phones, tablets, and desktops with both Day (Light) and Night (Dark) themes for comfortable viewing anywhere.
