Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026 • Website: https://ilovescalc.com

At ilovescalc.com, your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by ilovescalc.com and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Calculations & Local Storage

All calculators on ilovescalc.com execute 100% locally inside your web browser. We do not collect, transmit, or store the financial, health, or personal figures you enter into any calculator on our servers.

To enhance your experience, iLoveCalc uses your browser's localStorage solely to remember your Day/Night theme preference and your last 10 saved calculations. You can clear this data at any time using the "Clear All" button in the History panel or through your browser settings.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like most standard websites, our hosting infrastructure may record basic non-personally identifiable log files (such as browser type, referring/exit pages, and timestamps) for security, performance monitoring, and aggregate traffic analysis.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Cookies

Advertising is not currently enabled on this website. If we enable Google AdSense or another advertising service in the future, that provider may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertisements in accordance with its own policies and applicable law. This section will be updated before advertising is activated.

  • When advertising is enabled, the advertising provider's technologies may be used to serve or measure ads, subject to user settings and applicable consent requirements.
  • Users can review or adjust Google advertising personalization settings by visiting Google Ad Settings or www.aboutads.info.

4. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, delete, or restrict the processing of personal data and to opt out of personalized advertising cookies. Because iLoveCalc does not maintain user accounts or server-side databases of your inputs, no personal calculation records exist on our servers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@ilovescalc.com.
