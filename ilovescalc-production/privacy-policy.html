Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026 • Website: https://ilovescalc.com

At iLoveCalc.com, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

By using iLoveCalc.com, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully to understand our privacy practices.

1. Client-Side Calculations & Local Storage

Our calculators are designed to perform calculations directly inside your web browser. The values you enter into calculator fields are processed locally by the calculator functionality and are not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

iLoveCalc does not intentionally collect or maintain server-side records of the financial, health, mathematical, or personal figures entered into its calculators.

To improve your experience, our website may use your browser's localStorage to remember your Day/Night theme preference and your last 10 saved calculations.

This information remains in your browser unless you clear it through the available History panel controls or your browser settings. Clearing browser storage may remove saved preferences and calculation history.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal, medical, or financial information into website calculators.

2. Log Files & Website Usage

Like many websites, our hosting infrastructure may automatically record basic technical information when visitors access the site.

This information may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Internet service provider information
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Date and time of website visits
  • General website usage and diagnostic information

This information may be used for website security, technical troubleshooting, performance monitoring, and understanding general website traffic.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

iLoveCalc.com and certain third-party service providers may use cookies, web beacons, pixels, local storage, or similar technologies.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Maintain website functionality and preferences
  • Understand website usage and performance
  • Improve the user experience
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Support security and technical operations

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

iLoveCalc.com may use Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements on our website.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device information, or similar technologies to collect information when advertisements are displayed or interacted with.

These technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Displaying advertisements on our website
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Limiting how frequently advertisements are displayed
  • Detecting invalid advertising activity
  • Providing personalized or non-personalized advertisements, where applicable

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Advertising personalization and the use of advertising cookies may depend on user preferences, applicable consent requirements, and the settings of the advertising services.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, security, performance, analytics, and advertising.

These services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, and website usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We do not control how independent third-party providers collect or process information. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of those providers.

6. How We Use Information

Information processed through our website or its service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website
  • Improve calculator functionality and user experience
  • Monitor website performance and security
  • Identify and resolve technical problems
  • Understand general visitor activity
  • Support advertising and measure advertisement performance

We do not intentionally sell personal information submitted through calculator inputs.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect our website and the information processed through it.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

iLoveCalc.com is a general-purpose calculator website and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

9. GDPR, CCPA and Other Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have certain privacy rights under applicable data protection laws. These rights may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information
  • Requesting correction or deletion of personal information
  • Requesting restriction of certain data processing
  • Objecting to certain types of processing
  • Managing or opting out of certain advertising and tracking activities

iLoveCalc does not intentionally maintain server-side databases of calculator inputs. However, technical information may be processed by hosting, security, and advertising providers.

If you wish to exercise an applicable privacy right or have a privacy-related question, please contact us using the information provided below.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent and privacy controls may be provided for relevant visitors.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage your privacy preferences in several ways:

  • Manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • Manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.
  • Use available consent and privacy controls where applicable.
  • Clear saved calculator history and theme preferences from your browser.
  • Contact us regarding applicable privacy requests.

11. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, services, or resources.

We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of external websites. Visiting those websites is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, advertising practices, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
